About

Government Polytechnic Ghosi is a technical collegelocated in Mau, Uttar Pradesh. Government Polytechnic Mau is a top technical college in Uttar Pradesh, committed to providing outstanding education and developing talented professionals. Founded with the mission of providing industry-specific knowledge, the college provides an active learning environment that promotes innovation, creativity, and hands-on experience. With affiliation from the Board of Technical Education, Uttar Pradesh (BTEUP) and All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE) approval, Government Polytechnic Mau is dedicated to providing academic excellence. Our institution has always been known for awarding the best results and providing 100% placements to students, and is therefore one of the top polytechnic institutions in the region.
